You are given a list of integers representing the elevation of a terrain. Your task is to compute the volume of water that can be trapped between these elevations after raining. The water trapped at each position is determined by the heights of the tallest elevations to the left and right of that position.

Example 1:
Input:
heights = [0, 1, 0, 2, 1, 0, 1, 3, 2, 1, 2, 1]
Output: 6
Explanation:
The structure in the elevation array can trap 6 units of rain water.

Example 2:
Input:
heights = [4, 2, 0, 3, 2, 5]
Output: 9
Explanation:
The structure in the elevation array can trap 9 units of rain water.

Your Task:
You don't need to read input or print anything. Your task is to implement the function calculateTrappedWater() which takes a list of integers (heights) and returns the total volume of water that can be trapped.

Expected Time Complexity: O(n)
Expected Auxiliary Space: O(1)

Constraints:
1 <= len(heights) <= 10^5
0 <= heights[i] <= 10^4
